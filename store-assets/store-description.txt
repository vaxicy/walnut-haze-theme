Walnut Haze is a muted, earthy Chrome theme built on a natural neutral palette: a walnut-plum frame, a deeper plum toolbar, and a soft greige New Tab Page warmed by sand and khaki accents. Cream ink keeps every tab, bookmark and toolbar icon crisp against the dark chrome, while deep plum text and links keep the pale page easy to read. A single-row shortcut layout and a color-matched Google logo keep the page clean and uncluttered. Solid colors only, no wallpaper — quiet, natural, and easy on the eyes.
